## Architecture Summary
- **Single Source of Truth**: All pages reside in `content/landing-pages/`.
- **Dynamic Catch-All Route**: Rendered through `app/[slug]/page.tsx` using `generateStaticParams()` and `generateMetadata()`.
- **Future Additions**: Adding page #11 or #20 requires only adding a typed data object to `content/landing-pages/` — **zero hardcoded page boilerplate required**.
- **Sitemap**: Automatically synced via `app/sitemap.ts`.
- **Global Crawlability**: Linked across the homepage (`PopularSolutionsStrip`) and global footer (`Specialized Solutions & Regional Hubs`).
