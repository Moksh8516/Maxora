# Commercial & Engagement Model — Maxora Technology

> Maxora Technology builds custom software, web applications, mobile apps, CRM/ERP platforms, and search & AI visibility solutions. Every project is scoped individually based on your technical requirements, deliverables, and operational workflows. We operate on milestone-driven sprint pricing with 100% intellectual property (IP) and code ownership transfer to your team. There are zero recurring monthly per-seat licensing fees.

## Overview & Commercial Principles

- **Requirement-Based Scoping**: We do not force clients into rigid packages or inflated tiers. Every solution is architected and quoted specifically around your technical scope, user scale, and business goals.
- **Zero Per-Seat Monthly Licensing Fees**: Unlike SaaS platforms that charge escalating fees per user per month, software built by Maxora is owned outright by you. You host it on your own cloud infrastructure (AWS, Vercel, Google Cloud, Supabase, etc.).
- **100% Intellectual Property Ownership**: Full source code, database schemas, API credentials, and deployment scripts are transferred to your repository upon milestone completion.
- **Transparent 4-Step Milestone Process**: Discovery -> Architecture & Scoping -> Sprint Development -> Deployment & Handover.
- **Direct Engineer Access**: Clients communicate directly with full-stack engineers without account manager layers.
